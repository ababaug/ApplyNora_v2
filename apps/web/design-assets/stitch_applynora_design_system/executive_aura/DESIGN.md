## Brand & Style

This design system delivers an intelligent, authoritative, and empowering experience tailored for high-caliber job seekers, executives, and modern professionals navigating complex career transitions with AI assistance. It balances technological capability with human empathy, projecting precision, high craft, and absolute discretion.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Corporate SaaS** with nuanced **Glassmorphism and Tonal Layering**:
- **Clarity over Clutter**: High data density balanced by disciplined white space and airy typographic rhythm.
- **Luminescent Accents**: Rich amethyst and radiant magenta gradients inject vitality and forward momentum into an otherwise calm, neutral canvas.
- **Human-Centric Professionalism**: Avoids sterile AI tropes; uses warm rose undertones and crisp slate neutrals to foster reassurance and momentum during high-stakes job search journeys.

## Colors

The color system establishes a distinct visual identity centered on deep plum and luminous fuchsia accents, anchored by Slate 900 for high-contrast legibility.

- **Primary (`#8E1EA2`)**: Used for key interactive triggers, active navigation items, brand anchor elements, and primary call-to-action fills.
- **Secondary (`#C654C3`)**: Applied to AI suggestion highlights, secondary interactive states, and gradient blends paired with the primary hue.
- **Tertiary (`#ED96D7`)**: Reserved for decorative badges, active indicator rings, and soft focus states.
- **Surface Blush (`#FFC0DE` & `#FFF5F9`)**: Used as an ambient tint for container backgrounds, resume review callout panels, and elevated highlight states.
- **Neutral Core (`#1E293B` Slate 900)**: Serves as the primary typography color, delivering high-contrast, fatigue-free reading across dense application trackers and document previews.
- **Border Lavender (`#E2E0EC`)**: Provides crisp separation without the harshness of pure gray borders.

## Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans is specified across all levels for its clean geometry, approachable curves, and exceptional readability in dense user interfaces.

- **Headlines**: Tight letter-spacing and firm weights (`700` and `800`) establish structural authority on executive dashboards and marketing sections.
- **Body & Data Tables**: `body-md` (0.9375rem / 15px) provides optimal density for ATS parsing screens, tracker feeds, and cover letter editors without compromising optical comfort.
- **Labels & Badges**: Set in semi-bold (`600`) with intentional tracking (`0.01em` to `0.025em`) to retain legibility at small scale within status pills and metric cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard 12-column responsive fluid grid designed to accommodate wide data views, multi-pane candidate workspaces, and asymmetric marketing hero sections.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`< 768px`), Tablet (`768px - 1024px`), Desktop (`1025px - 1440px`), and Ultrawide (`> 1440px`).
- **Margins & Gutters**: Outer canvas margins use `margin-mobile` (1rem) on handheld screens, expanding to `margin` (2rem) on tablets and `margin-desktop` (3rem) on large displays. Gutters remain tight (`1rem` on mobile, `1.5rem` on desktop) to support dense side-by-side split views (e.g., resume editor beside ATS feedback).
- **Rhythm**: Internal component spacing uses increments of 4px and 8px via `space-xs` through `space-xl`, ensuring precise alignment between icon-text pairs, card interiors, and table rows.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined blend of subtle surface tints, delicate lavender borders, and low-opacity chromatic shadows.

- **Canvas Ground**: Set to `#F8FAFC`, creating crisp separation from elevated pure white surfaces.
- **Card Surfaces**: Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a 1px border of `#E2E0EC`. No heavy dark shadows are used.
- **Level 1 Elevation (Dashboard Cards & Panels)**: A soft, diffused drop shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04), 0 4px 16px -4px rgba(142, 30, 162, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 Elevation (Hover States & Dropdowns)**: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(142, 30, 162, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 Elevation (Modals & Command Palettes)**: A backdrop blur filter (`backdrop-blur-md` with `rgba(248, 250, 252, 0.8)`) complemented by `0 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.16)`.
- **Chromatic Aura**: Key AI suggestion cards and active status widgets feature an ambient inward glow or border gradient derived from `#8E1EA2` transitioning to `#C654C3`.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** approach to instill modern friendliness while retaining crisp corporate discipline.

- **Base Radius (0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard for form fields, buttons, table cell selections, and dropdown menus.
- **Large Radius (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px)**: Applied to metric widgets, application stage cards, and modal dialogs.
- **Pill Radius (`rounded-full`)**: Strictly reserved for status badges, counter chips, avatar frames, and floating action indicators.
- **Icon Enclosures**: Soft rounded squares (8px radius) using `#FFF5F9` blush tints to house plum-colored action icons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid background using a vibrant gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #8E1EA2 0%, #C654C3 100%)`. High-contrast white text, subtle hover lift, and active scale compression (0.98).
- **Secondary**: Outlined with `#E2E0EC`, pure white surface, `#1E293B` text. On hover, background shifts to `#FFF5F9` and border transitions to `#C654C3`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary**: Transparent surface with `#8E1EA2` text, transitioning to a soft lavender fill on hover.

### Badges & Status Chips
- **Verified / Match Active**: Light emerald background (`#ECFDF5`) with `#059669` text and dot indicator.
- **AI Suggested**: Soft blush tint (`#FFF5F9`) with `#8E1EA2` text and subtle `#ED96D7` border.
- **Pending / In Review**: Amber tint (`#FFFBEB`) with `#D97706` text.
- **Neutral / Draft**: Slate wash (`#F1F5F9`) with `#64748B` text.

### Form Inputs & Selects
- 1px border using `#E2E0EC` on pure white containers.
- **Focus State**: Replaces default ring with a sharp 2px glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 84, 195, 0.2)` and border color `#8E1EA2`.
- Helper and error text positioned with strict 4px spacing below fields.

### Cards & Telemetry Counters
- Metric cards showcase large bold values (`headline-lg`) paired with micro-labels (`label-sm`).
- Include upward/downward trajectory tags styled with micro pill badges.
- Cards maintain internal padding of `space-lg` (1.5rem) with subtle top-border accent options using the brand gradient.

### Tables & Application Trackers
- Sticky header rows with `#F8FAFC` background and micro-uppercase labels (`caption`).
- Row hover states transition to `#FFF5F9` at 50% opacity, maintaining clear horizontal borders of `#E2E0EC`.
